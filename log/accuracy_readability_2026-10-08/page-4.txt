5 Conclusion 204
Writing calculations limits answers suggested by 205
misleading names in almost every tested arith- 206
metic contrast. The paired renaming test measures 207
resistance to a competing word while holding the 208
equations ﬁxed. The internal explanation remains 209
unresolved: the probe association depends on one 210
model, whose generated-chain readouts fail cali- 211
bration even after retraining. 212
Limitations 213
• Scope. Short synthetic arithmetic with En- 214
glish number words does not establish real- 215
code effects. Identiﬁer-style names cover one 216
level. Models are at most 12B, with raw 217
completion prompts; chat templates and long- 218
form reasoning are untested. Accuracy varies 219
with demonstrations, so behavioral claims re- 220
quire agreement across three sets. 221
• Internal measurements. Probes establish 222
decodability, not use. Most measurements 223
supply correct chains. Generated-chain cal- 224
ibration passes for Llama-3.2-3B but fails 225
for OLMo-2-1B, even after retraining (Ap- 226
pendix N); only two models are tested. 227
The nine-model correlation is descriptive, re- 228
verses without OLMo, and confounds value 229
availability with competence; variable roles 230
are not independent models. 231
• Interventions. Patches replace mixed repre- 232
sentations and damage correct answers. CoT 233
also repeats names and equations; the value- 234
only format changes performance and is not 235
an equivalent intervention (Appendix H). 236

5 Conclusion 206
Writing calculations limits answers suggested by 207
misleading names in almost every tested arith- 208
metic contrast. The paired renaming test measures 209
resistance to a competing word while holding the 210
equations ﬁxed. The internal explanation remains 211
unresolved: the probe association depends on one 212
model, whose generated-chain readouts fail cali- 213
bration even after retraining. 214
Limitations 215
• Scope. Short synthetic arithmetic with En- 216
glish number words does not establish real- 217
code effects. Identiﬁer-style names cover one 218
level. Models are at most 12B, with raw 219
completion prompts; chat templates and long- 220
form reasoning are untested. Accuracy varies 221
with demonstrations, so behavioral claims re- 222
quire agreement across three sets. 223
• Internal measurements. Probes establish 224
decodability, not use. Most measurements 225
supply correct chains. Generated-chain cal- 226
ibration passes for Llama-3.2-3B but fails 227
for OLMo-2-1B, even after retraining (Ap- 228
pendix N); only two models are tested. 229
The nine-model correlation is descriptive, re- 230
verses without OLMo, and confounds value 231
availability with competence; variable roles 232
are not independent models. 233
• Interventions. Patches replace mixed repre- 234
sentations and damage correct answers. CoT 235

# Combined eight-page version

**Correct Values Can Be Recovered Before Wrong Reasoning Steps**

The combined draft is in [paper/combined](../paper/combined/README.md):
[PDF](../paper/combined/main.pdf), [LaTeX](../paper/combined/main.tex) and
[source archive](../paper/combined/submission_source.zip).

The argument follows the gap between recovering a correct value and writing it.
The opening example shows how the demonstration change corrects a model's written
values and final answer. The recovery figure compares internal readouts before
writing and immediately before the same wrong outputs. The accuracy table shows
that the change improves intermediate writes and final answers on the same
programs. Arithmetic supplies the connection to Kudo et al. and a comparison;
its probe qualifications and secondary controls are mainly in the appendix.

The operation-control and fresh-name figures remain in the main paper. The
original selection deviation is explicit, and the probes concern the original
cohort. The eight-page limit includes Limitations; references and appendices follow.

Both short manuscripts remain available. This draft consolidates their evidence into
one long-paper alternative and removes the reciprocal companion-paper citations.
